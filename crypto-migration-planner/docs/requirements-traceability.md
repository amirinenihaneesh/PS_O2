## Processor Information Actually Present

The IBM guideline's printed page 4 gives high-level descriptions only: Processor A is summarized as 5 qubits, a fixed-baseline graph, and synthetic noise; Processor B as 7 qubits and a heavy-hex-inspired graph. These are not machine-readable definitions and do not supply exact coupling edges, basis gates, noise parameters, benchmark APIs, or versions. The Challenge Guide's processor JSON is explicitly illustrative (printed pp. 14-15), not an A/B configuration.

## Prototype Boundary

Until the blocked organizer requirements are supplied, project behavior is a demonstration only. It is not an official PS-P2 implementation, does not establish hackathon compliance, and does not produce official Processor A/B results. No score or PQC replacement mapping is inferred from the problem title.
